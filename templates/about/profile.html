PCE Consulting and Engineers LLP is a Mumbai-based engineering consultancy focusing on Technical Services, Manpower Outsourcing, and Engineering Training in Process Engineering & Equipment Design, Piping Engineering, and Instrumentation & Electrical Engineering.

We provide detailed engineering consultancy and solutions to process plants across Oil & Gas, Refineries, Petrochemicals, Fertilizers, Chemicals, and Skid-based systems.

Our diverse customer portfolio reflects our strong industry presence and the confidence clients place in our expertise and delivery capabilities.

PCE Consulting and engineers LLP business solutions provide a foundation upon which organizations can implement solutions that enable them to better manage information and streamline their business processes.

PCE delivers services supported by high-end engineering software including Intergraph Smart Plant P&ID, SPI (IN-Tools), PV Elite, AutoCAD, and MicroStation. We also support pre-bid engineering, detailed engineering, inspection, and site supervision.

OUR STATS

We provide skilled and technical manpower for various project execution. We have bulk of expertise engineers from all disciplines.

We also undertake projects at client location/site. This helps clients to retain valuable data within premises & effectively monitor schedule with proper coordination within project teams.

PCE Consulting & Engineers LLP provides innovative engineering, automation and industrial solutions with a commitment to quality, safety and customer satisfaction.
